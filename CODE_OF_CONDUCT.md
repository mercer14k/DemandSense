# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Challenge ideas with evidence; do not attack people. Welcome different levels of experience and perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not share private information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, pause a discussion, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate, explained where safe, and consistent. Report concerns privately using a maintainer's published contact channel. Protect the privacy of everyone involved.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces.
